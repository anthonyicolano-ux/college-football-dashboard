How to read the logs and recent form

Each log lists a completed team game against an FBS opponent. Dates show the source kickoff date in UTC; late evening local games may have the next UTC date. No recorded offensive stats means the source has no recognized passing, rushing or receiving statistics for this player in that game. It does not establish whether the player appeared, was injured or played zero snaps. Missing values stay —; they are not converted to zero.

Season, Last 3 and Last Game use the same team-game windows as Player Stats. Per-game averages divide by games with recorded offensive statistics, not all team games or confirmed appearances. If a field is missing in any recorded game in a window, its aggregate stays unavailable. Last Game may have no recorded stats even when earlier games do.

Carry share and reception share compare player counts with matching team counts. Window shares use combined counts in the player's recorded games, rather than averaging game percentages. Game logs display each game's own share. Yards per attempt/carry/reception also use combined counts in summary windows. Passing and rushing yardage follows source box-score conventions, including sack treatment where applicable.

The recent windows overlap the season; they are descriptive, not independent forecasts or a trend model. TD/G is touchdowns divided by recorded games, while game-log touchdowns are counts. Targets remain deferred. Logs use current roster positions and don't infer starters or snap counts.
