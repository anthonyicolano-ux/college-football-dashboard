How to read this matchup

Attempts/G describes volume. Efficiency vs expected compares yards with opponent-specific expected yards at the actual observed attempt counts. For offense, above 100% means more efficient than opponents normally allowed; for defense, below 100% means opponents were less efficient than usual. Adjusted efficiency rank is separate from adjusted yards rank. Attempts vs baseline describes volume relative to opponents’ other-game attempts/G. It is not inherently good or bad.

Min opponent sample is the smallest other-game count behind yardage expectations. Usable efficiency games may differ from usable yardage games. Partial efficiency comparisons show the percentage but have no efficiency rank. These metrics do not measure plays per minute, true snap counts, or calibrated confidence.

Player production and usage are shown alongside opponent-adjusted team offense and the opposing defense. Defense labels describe the defense only. They are not player projections or combined offense-versus-defense ratings. Raw rank 1 is strongest; defensive strength labels use the top, middle and bottom thirds of eligible raw ranks.

Offense adjusted % is offensive yards divided by what opponents allowed in their other eligible games, multiplied by 100. Above 100% means more production than the opponent allowance baseline. Offensive raw ranks use yards/game, highest first; adjusted ranks use adjusted %, highest first.

Defense adjusted % compares actual yards allowed with expected production from opponents' other games. Below 100% means fewer yards allowed than expected. Adjusted ranks require full baseline coverage. A defense with fewer than three selected games is flagged as a limited sample.

Each team's Last 3/Last Game window is its own latest completed FBS-versus-FBS games. Player games mean games with recorded offensive stats, not confirmed appearances. Usage shares compare matching recorded games. Missing fields remain —. Targets are deferred and are not used in this view.

Upcoming games are those listed as incomplete with future kickoff dates at the schedule refresh. The page hides known past kickoff times. Kickoff dates with TBD times have no confirmed time, and schedules may change between refreshes. Manual comparisons do not imply a scheduled game. Offense and defense adjustments use total actual divided by total expected over the same usable games. The current game is excluded from its opponent baseline. Each opponent needs at least one other eligible game; full coverage is required for adjusted ranks. These are descriptive current-season comparisons, not historical pregame backtests. The two percentages are shown separately; no combined score or predicted yards are calculated. The model does not fully adjust for pace, game script or opponents' own schedule strength.

About & data sources

Data supplied through CollegeFootballData. This dashboard calculates rankings, adjustment percentages and usage shares. Team Defense and Player Stats use their existing datasets; the schedule updater publishes only upcoming-game fields required by this page. Refresh dates are listed above. Data may be delayed.

Stat coverage and missing values

Expand Stat coverage to see how many selected games reported each field. Team coverage uses selected FBS games; player coverage uses games with recorded offensive stats. A reported zero counts as available. Missing categories do not establish appearances or zero production. Optional totals remain unavailable if any recorded game's field is missing; partial opponent adjustments can show percentages without a rank. Complete reported inputs can still have no rate when the denominator is zero. Targets remain deferred and are omitted from coverage completeness labels.
